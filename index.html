Meet the Founder — Uriel Adesunkanmi

My name is Uriel Adesunkanmi. I'm an ambitious individual with dreams to chase and a mission to make every moment, every choice and every step count.

I started YIUBOI with a simple question: Why should customers pay so much for products that fail to deliver the quality and satisfaction they deserve?

I believe customers deserve better. That's why YIUBOI exists — to bring together style, quality and value, while making sure every customer feels satisfied with their choice.
